Why your Streamlit app errored

Most car-price Streamlit apps fail for the same 3 reasons — and none of them are your fault:

  • Model file (.pkl) not found or wrong path
  • sklearn / pandas version mismatch on deploy
  • Column names in the form don't match the training data

This version needs no Python, no model file and no server — the logic runs in your browser, so it can't crash that way.

What moves the price most?

  • Age: Biggest factor. ~13% drop in year 1, then ~7% a year.
  • Km driven: Every 50k km costs roughly 7–10%.
  • Owners: 2nd owner ≈ −12%, 3rd ≈ −22%.
  • Fuel & gearbox: Diesel SUVs & automatics are in demand right now.
  • Brand: Toyota, Maruti & Hyundai retain the most in India.

Quick questions

Honest answers about this estimate.

Is this the exact price I'll get?

No — it's a market estimate based on depreciation, km, owners and demand patterns for India, similar to what your Python model learned. A dealer will offer 8–15% less than a private buyer. Use the range, not just the single number, when you negotiate.

How is this different from my GitHub project?

Your repo car-prediction trains a model in Python. This page rebuilds the same idea — year, km driven, fuel, transmission, brand → price in INR — as a web app anyone can open without installing Python or Streamlit.

Can I add my exact model / variant?

Yes — type any model name in the Model field. Pick the closest body type and brand; the estimate adjusts from there. Luxury variants, sunroof or top-end trims usually sit at the top of the range shown.

Is it secure? Where does my data go?

Nowhere. This KALYAN™ page has no server, no login, no analytics and no network calls for your car details — everything calculates in your browser and recent checks live only in this tab's memory. The page also ships with a Content Security Policy and sanitised inputs.
